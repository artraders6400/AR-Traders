1. Return Eligibility

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery under the following conditions:

  • The saree is unused, unwashed, and in original condition
  • Original tags and packaging are intact
  • You have the receipt or proof of purchase
  • The saree has a manufacturing defect (verified by our team)

2. Non-Returnable Items

  • Sarees that have been worn, washed, or altered
  • Items damaged due to customer mishandling
  • Discounted/sale items (unless defective)
  • Custom or personalized orders

3. Return Process

  • WhatsApp us at +91 [YOUR_WHATSAPP_NUMBER] within 7 days of delivery
